Privacy Policy

Table Copy Chrome extension. Last updated October 14, 2026.

Table Copy does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device.

What the extension does

When you right-click a table and choose a format (or use a keyboard shortcut), Table Copy reads that one table on the page you are viewing, converts it to Markdown, CSV, TSV or JSON on your device, and puts the result on your clipboard.

Data we collect

None. Table Copy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It makes no network requests, and the content of the pages you visit and the tables you copy is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Table Copy saves four option settings (keep links in Markdown, align Markdown columns, remove empty rows and columns, CSV separator) using Chrome's storage.sync. These settings may sync across your own Chrome profile through your Google account. They never include page content, table content, or browsing history.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Context menus: to add the "Copy table as…" items to the right-click menu.
  • Clipboard write: to place the converted table on your clipboard when you ask. The extension never reads your clipboard.
  • Storage: to remember your option settings.
  • Access to all websites (content script): tables can appear on any site, so the extension needs to run on all pages to know which table you right-clicked. It only reads a table when you choose a copy action, and it does not modify pages.

Remote code

Table Copy does not load or run remote code. All of its JavaScript is included in the extension package.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer user data to anyone, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, for example by adding optional paid features that involve a payment provider, this policy will be updated before the change is released and the date above will change.
